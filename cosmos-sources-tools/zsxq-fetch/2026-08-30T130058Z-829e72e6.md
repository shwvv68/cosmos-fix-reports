## Why the change was needed

The authenticated timeline collector rejected a supported top-level Word attachment as a file-inventory mismatch. The expected behavior is to inventory, officially download, extract, verify, and render `.docx` attachments without dropping the containing topic.

## Reproduction

1. Open an authenticated timeline containing a target-date topic whose only attachment is a Word `.docx` file.
2. Run the strict timeline collector and submit its inventory to the runner.
3. Before the repair the collector returned `FILE_ATTACHMENT_MISMATCH`; after the repair it emits a typed DOCX inventory record and the runner accepts a verified local representation.
